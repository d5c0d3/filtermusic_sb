SETUP_PLUGIN_FILTERMUSIC_CACHETTL
	EN	Cache Duration

SETUP_PLUGIN_FILTERMUSIC_CACHETTL_DESC
	EN	How long (in minutes) the station list itself is kept before being re-read from filtermusic.net. FilterMusic is still checked every time you enter its menu regardless of this setting, to pick up a fresh background photo; this only controls how long the (much larger, slower-changing) list of stations is reused rather than re-read.

SETUP_PLUGIN_FILTERMUSIC_BACKDROP
	EN	Background Photo (Material Skin)

SETUP_PLUGIN_FILTERMUSIC_BACKDROP_DESC
	EN	Show filtermusic.net's current wallpaper photo as the background when browsing FilterMusic categories in Material Skin. A new photo is picked each time you enter the FilterMusic menu, same as a full page reload on filtermusic.net itself, and stays fixed until you leave and come back. Requires "Draw background" to also be enabled in Material Skin's own Interface settings. Has no effect on other skins, apps, or players.
